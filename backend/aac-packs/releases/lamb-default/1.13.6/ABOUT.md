## What this version is for

This is the knowledge pack that ships with LAMB 0.9. It gives LEGATUS its way of working with an educator: how it greets, how it asks before it changes anything, the vocabulary it uses in four languages, and a set of guides for the tasks educators ask for most.

A guide is advice. LEGATUS reads the guide that fits a task and follows it. When no guide fits, LEGATUS can still use any tool your LAMB offers to your role, and it still asks you before every change.

## What changed

This is the first pack published as a line. Until now an organization chose among many numbered versions; from LAMB 0.9 it follows the stable pack, and older versions are no longer installed.

The most recent additions, in the order they arrived:

- **Prompt filters** can be previewed and set on an assistant from a conversation.
- **Auto mode**: LEGATUS prepares a plan for a longer task, asks once, and saves its results so that a conversation can continue later.
- **Activity analysis**: LEGATUS studies the conversations of a published assistant, proposes what to look at, and saves a report that names its evidence and says when the evidence is incomplete.
- **Moodle courses** can be named by part of their name or through a learning scenario.

## Languages

LEGATUS answers in English, Spanish, Catalan or Basque, with a vocabulary checked against the words of the LAMB interface in each language.

## Needs

LAMB 0.8.0 or later. The Moodle guides appear only to a creator who has connected Moodle.
