# Cellular Respiration — Course Notes, Week 4

Cellular respiration releases the energy stored in glucose and captures it as ATP.

## Stages

**Glycolysis**, in the cytoplasm, splits glucose into two pyruvate molecules and
yields a small net amount of ATP. It requires no oxygen.

**The Krebs cycle**, in the mitochondrial matrix, oxidises pyruvate derivatives
completely to carbon dioxide, filling the electron carriers NADH and FADH2.

**Oxidative phosphorylation**, at the inner mitochondrial membrane, uses those
carriers to drive a proton gradient, and ATP synthase converts that gradient into
the great majority of the cell's ATP.

## Relationship to photosynthesis

The two processes are near mirror images: photosynthesis stores energy and
releases oxygen, respiration consumes oxygen and releases stored energy.
