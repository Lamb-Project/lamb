# Biology 101 — Syllabus

## Assessment

Continuous assessment is 40% of the final mark; the final examination is 60%.
A student must reach 4.0 out of 10 in the examination for the continuous mark
to count.

## Schedule

- Week 1–2: cell structure
- Week 3: photosynthesis
- Week 4: cellular respiration
- Week 5: laboratory — microscopy
- Week 6: mid-term examination
